# Philippine Internet Service Providers (ISPs)

This is a comprehensive list of Internet Service Providers operating in the Philippines.

## Major National ISPs

### Fiber and DSL Providers

1. **PLDT (Philippine Long Distance Telephone Company)**
   - One of the largest telecommunications companies in the Philippines
   - Offers DSL, Fiber, and wireless internet services
   - Brand: PLDT Home

2. **Globe Telecom**
   - Major telecommunications provider
   - Offers fiber, DSL, and mobile broadband services
   - Brand: Globe At Home

3. **Converge ICT Solutions Inc.**
   - Rapidly growing fiber internet provider
   - Pure fiber internet services
   - Known for competitive pricing

4. **Sky Broadband (SkyCable)**
   - Cable and fiber internet provider
   - Part of ABS-CBN Corporation
   - Offers cable internet and fiber services

5. **Eastern Communications (PLDT subsidiary)**
   - Enterprise and residential internet services
   - Fiber optic network provider
   - Focus on business solutions

### Cable Internet Providers

6. **Philippine Cable and Telecommunications (PCTA)**
   - Cable television and internet services
   - Regional coverage

7. **Destiny Cable**
   - Cable TV and internet provider
   - Operating in various regions

### Mobile/Wireless Internet Providers

8. **Smart Communications (PLDT subsidiary)**
   - Mobile network operator
   - Offers mobile broadband and wireless home internet
   - LTE and 5G services

9. **Globe Telecom Mobile**
   - Mobile internet and broadband services
   - LTE and 5G network coverage

10. **DITO Telecommunity**
    - Third major telecommunications player
    - Mobile and wireless internet services
    - Newest major telco entrant

### Regional and Local ISPs

11. **Bayantel**
    - Fixed-line and internet services
    - Now owned by Globe Telecom

12. **Now Telecom**
    - Fiber and wireless internet provider
    - Focuses on Metro Manila and surrounding areas

13. **Air Internet**
    - Wireless internet service provider
    - Serves various areas with fixed wireless

14. **Philippine Global Communications (Philcom)**
    - Enterprise and consumer internet services
    - Infrastructure provider

15. **Radius Telecoms**
    - Wireless broadband provider
    - Regional coverage

16. **Websky Communications**
    - Internet service provider
    - Regional operations

17. **iConnect**
    - Internet service provider
    - Serves specific localities

18. **Innove Communications**
    - Regional fiber and wireless ISP
    - Growing fiber network

19. **Streamtech Systems**
    - Regional ISP
    - Fixed wireless and fiber services

20. **WiFi Vendo Providers**
    - Various local community-based internet providers
    - Coin-operated WiFi services

## Business and Enterprise ISPs

21. **PLDT Enterprise**
    - Business-focused internet solutions
    - Dedicated fiber and managed services

22. **Globe Business**
    - Enterprise connectivity solutions
    - Cloud and managed services

23. **Eastern Telecoms**
    - Business internet and data center services
    - Carrier-neutral solutions

## Notes

- The telecommunications industry in the Philippines is regulated by the National Telecommunications Commission (NTC)
- Coverage and availability vary by region
- Major urban areas have more ISP options compared to rural areas
- The industry has seen increased competition with the entry of new players like Converge and DITO
